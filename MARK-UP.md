# Zahari — Plain-Language Product Summary

Updated: 3 November 2026

Website: https://zahari.ink

Zahari is a subscription art experience built around crypto-inspired miniature worlds. People choose coins they care about and explore each coin's distinctive 3D subject: a fortress, castle, villa, aircraft, or boat. Historical market data influences its appearance and surroundings. Higher subscriptions also include brief, source-linked news stories.

The appeal is beauty, curiosity, attachment to a chosen coin's identity, and having something enjoyable to revisit. It does not promise profitable trades or predict future prices.

## What someone does in the app

1. Explore the public sample without paying or providing a card.
2. Create a username-and-password account and, if interested, choose a monthly membership paid by card.
3. Select the coins included in that membership and open their worlds.
4. Rotate the artwork, zoom in, choose a lighting mood, or save a screenshot.
5. Return after a scheduled refresh to discover the selected visual state. A refresh can produce the same state again.

The public sample demonstrates the viewing experience. It allows manual switching between lowland, sea, and sky. In paid coin worlds, the location is chosen by the data; the visitor controls the camera and lighting.

Membership provides access to the selected worlds while the subscription is active. A crypto wallet is not required.

## How the worlds change

The subject and its setting respond separately to the history. An impressive-looking subject can appear in an unsettled environment. There are four placements: lowland, partly buried lowland, sea, and sky. Their meaning depends on the subject:

| Subject family | Most settled conditions | Settling | Unsettled | Most turbulent |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Buildings: BNB, BTC, ETH, LINK | Lowland | Partly buried lowland | Sea | Sky |
| Boat: XAUT | Sea | Lowland | Partly buried lowland | Sky |
| Aircraft: AAVE, SOL, UNI | Sky | Sea | Lowland | Partly buried lowland |

These are artistic interpretations, not buy/sell ratings. The app selects finished artwork variants rather than generating an entirely new model at each refresh. The scenery adds atmosphere and movement around the subject.

Lighting begins with the visitor's device-local time: dawn from 6–9am, daylight from 9am–3pm, dusk from 3–7pm, and night otherwise. Visitors can always switch those four moods manually. Lighting time is independent of the news schedule.

## What the historical data means

The owner manually refreshes the available history for all eight coins using a chosen date range. Each upload runs once.

At a member's scheduled refresh, Zahari randomly selects a complete, chronological section of that coin's uploaded history. The plan determines the section's size. Only starting points with enough following observations are eligible.

Different members can see different states for the same coin and history. Each selection stays in place until recalculation. The refresh schedule begins when the member selects the coin.

The accurate description is **“market history shapes the world.”** The current selection method does not mean the artwork is a direct reading of the coin's latest price movement. Scheduled current news is a separate layer.

## Monthly membership

All prices are in US dollars and paid by card.

| Feature | One — $4.99/month | Five — $8.99/month | Eight — $12.99/month |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Coins selected at once | 1 | Up to 5 | Up to all 8 launch coins |
| World recalculation | Every 12 hours | Every 8 hours | Every 4 hours |
| Historical observations used per coin | 1,100 | 2,200 | 3,300 |
| News stories | Not included | Three scheduled daily editions | Three scheduled daily editions |
| Remove a coin to free a place | Not during the current paid billing cycle | Allowed | Allowed |
| Choose a different coin | After the next paid renewal | After freeing a place | After freeing a place |
| Camera, lighting, and screenshots | Included | Included | Included |

Observations come from several short intervals, so their count is not a fixed time span. Each coin needs enough history to fill its plan's complete window.

The free sample is separate from these memberships. It is not a free tier that unlocks a paid coin world. Members can manage or cancel their subscription from their profile.

## News in the world

The Five and Eight plans have the same news edition times: **9am, 1pm, and 9pm New York time**, including the local daylight-saving change.

For a selected coin, a qualifying story appears as a short summary in a backdrop banner, with a source link opening in a new tab. A banner lasts up to one hour. It can remain empty when no qualifying new story is available.

Searches cover only coins selected by eligible paid members. Everyone choosing BTC shares its edition. Opening a viewer adds no searches; no eligible paid selections means no searches.

News supplies context beside the artwork. It does not currently choose or reshape the 3D subject.

## Business direction and intended audience

People pay for a personal collection of evolving coin worlds and reasons to return: appreciation, discovery, and attachment to a favourite subject. News adds practical value.

In the earlier “fountain versus tap” discussion, the intended direction was mainly fountain: an experience people enjoy spending time with. News contributes some tap-like usefulness. No exact percentage has been validated.

The first audience to test enjoys both crypto culture and attractive digital art, miniature environments, or collectible identities. Demand, repeat use, willingness to pay, and the best acquisition channel still need to be demonstrated.

The current offer is access to the artwork experience. NFT ownership, physical collectibles, trading signals, and an open-world game are not part of this release.

## Promotion discussed so far

The proposed first experiment is a faceless 15–30 second video: a short AI-assisted opening, actual Zahari screen recordings, narration, and captions. The hook is **“What if Bitcoin were a place you could visit?”** Show the fortress, demonstrate rotation and lighting, reveal the aircraft and boat, and invite viewers to the free sample.

Test Instagram Reels first and cross-post the clean edit to YouTube Shorts. Around $15 per DIY video was a discussion assumption, not a verified production quote or a purchase of audience reach.

Measure sample visits, signups, return visits, and subscriptions. The channel and creative are experiments; video views alone do not establish demand.
